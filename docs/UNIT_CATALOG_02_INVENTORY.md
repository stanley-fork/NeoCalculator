## Deferred

- dalton: Measured mass: uncertainty policy required.
- gallons, calories, horsepower: Variant catalogue deferred; avoid ambiguous names and rounded exactness.
- calendar months and years: No universal fixed duration; convention selector deferred.
- neper, bel, decibel: Logarithmic transformation support deferred.
- astronomical arcsecond prefixes: Standard mas, µas and pas are validated and formatted with as; the first catalogue offers only the neutral arcsecond.
- prefixed absolute Celsius: SI permits prefixes; the first catalogue offers neutral Celsius and explicit temperature differences pending affine quantity semantics.
